04 / HOW I APPROACH WORK

Building with care, simplicity, & safety.

A few core principles I keep in mind when designing backend systems and AI applications.

01 DESIGN FOR RELIABILITY

Validate queries and inputs before execution, handle errors cleanly, and add automated fallbacks so systems remain dependable.

02 KEEP HUMANS IN CONTROL

Consequential writes and external messages should always have a clear review step so people stay informed and in control.

03 MEASURE & OBSERVE

Keep track of latency, errors, and state transitions so issues can be diagnosed quickly without adding overhead to users.

04 FOCUS ON PRACTICAL VALUE

Focus on solving real everyday problems for users and teams rather than using complexity for its own sake.
